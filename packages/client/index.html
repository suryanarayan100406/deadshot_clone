How to play

Move with WASD, look with the mouse, shoot with Mouse 1. First to the kill limit wins the round.

Movement

Air control is unrestricted — hold a strafe key and sweep the mouse the same way while airborne to gain speed. Chaining jumps is faster than walking, and moving targets are much harder to hit.

Shooting

Every gun blooms as you fire. Tapping keeps the cone tight; holding the trigger opens it up. Aiming down sights shrinks it hard but slows you down. Headshots do far more damage on everything except the shotgun.

Staying alive

Health regenerates after five quiet seconds. Break line of sight, let it come back, then re-engage. Falling from a great height hurts.
